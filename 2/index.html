Part 2: Fun with Frequencies

2.1 — Image "Sharpening"

WRITE: Gaussian = low-pass. image − blur = high frequencies. Sharpened = image + α·(image − blur). As one kernel: (1+α)·δ − α·G (the unsharp mask filter), which matches the 3-step result. Effect of α: higher α = crisper, then halos/noise.

2.3 — Gaussian and Laplacian Stacks

WRITE: Gaussian stack = repeatedly blur, no downsampling. Laplacian stack = G[i] − G[i+1] (one frequency band each) plus the last Gaussian level; it sums back to the original exactly (verified). Levels and σ used.

2.4 — Multiresolution Blending

WRITE: Blend each Laplacian level with the matching level of the mask's Gaussian stack, then sum. Fine bands get a sharp transition (no ghosting), coarse bands a wide one (no visible seam).
